# The UI redesign

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-15, on a design canvas (claude.ai artifact "WackCode 3 UI Redesign", https://claude.ai/artifact/FCzbBYRTkzpCUx6huFips9). The canvas is the picture to build toward. This file is what was decided and the order it lands in. Each phase is its own commit, checked in the gallery and the dev app, light and dark, over a picture and without one.

## Decisions

- **The window is a frame and a stage.** The sidebar and the stage are panes with an 8-point gutter around them, and the chat tabs sit on pills in the title strip between them.
- **Over a picture, the panes are frosted glass.** The picture fills the window, blurred and dimmed as the user chose; the owner wants to keep seeing it. The glass is never thinner than `theme::GLASS_MIN` and thickens only where text would not read. A picture behind just the title strip, with plain ground below it, was considered and rejected.
- **The user sets blur and dim** in Settings › Appearance, as in WackCode 2.
- **New chat and welcome: the picture is shown, not dimmed.** The whole stage shows it clear (optionally through a texture). The smaller words sit on frosted pills and the display line has a faint halo of the window's colour; nothing is laid over the picture (a feathered patch of glass behind the heading was tried and removed on the owner's call). When the first message is sent, the blur and dim ease in as the picture fades to the glass (and back on New chat). Decided 2026-11-15 after phase 1: a stage full of glass on the new-chat screen read as dimming.
- **Themes are chosen, never made.** The user picks one of a fixed set of themes, WackCode 2's (WackCode, Midnight, Lagoon, Verdant, Grape, Nebula, Rosé, Solar, Ember, Mono; four with gradient accents), plus light or dark. No hand-picked colours: the paint chips and hex fields go. Nothing taken from the picture overrides the theme. Decided 2026-11-15, reversing the earlier cut of theme presets.
- **Pills and circles are fine.** The serif is used sparingly: a screen's one display line, not every label.
- **Themes in light** derive from each theme's hue: the background's hue tints the light ramp as strongly as it tints the dark one, and the accent is deepened only where it is text. Decided 2026-11-15.
- **Gradient accents return** on the accent's fills for Lagoon, Verdant, Nebula and Solar; text, lines and focus stay the solid accent, and no sweep may read as Ultra's prism. Decided 2026-11-15.
- **A picture remembers its theme.** The picture library comes back: showing a picture brings back the theme last used with it. Decided 2026-11-15.
- **Composer:** round controls and a mode track. The model gets its own pill, and **effort gets its own pill** opening WackCode 2's slider (Low, Medium, High, Xhigh, Max, Ultra) rather than living inside the model menu. While a run is going, status pills sit above the composer (time and what it is doing, the todo list, the queue). Ultra Plan wears the prism around the composer.
- **Transcript:** the user's message is a bubble on the right. "Worked for 19s" is a chip with a dotted rule and a one-line summary, and opens into compact rows: a round icon, the verb, the target in mono, its meta. Diffs and code are sheets with a head band.
- **Settings:** grouped section navigation, sized for about 14 sections. A page opens with a title and its primary action, then cards of rows: label and description on the left, the control on the right. Connections are rows with a switch.

## Phases

1. **Frame, stage, glass, blur and dim** (done): `shell.rs`, `glass.rs`, `backdrop.rs`, `theme.rs` (`Theme::glass`), and the Blur choice on Settings › Appearance.
2. **Sidebar and title strip** (done): the New chat pill, round project monograms, chats on a dotted rail, tab pills with the sidebar's state marks and a new-tab button; and the new-chat screen showing the picture clear, easing to glass when a chat opens. The canvas's Search chats field and Archived button wait until those exist.
3. **Transcript** (done): bubbles, the work chip with its count of what was done, rows on a dotted rail with round icons, and sheets with a head band (code sheets copy). The canvas's scroll rail of prompt ticks and a reply's copy button wait for later.
4. **Composer** (done): round controls, the effort pill opening WackCode 2's slider (Default, then Low through Ultra; it stays open as it moves), status pills over the card (what the run is doing and for how long, the todo list and the waiting messages, which open above their pills), and the prism's edge in Ultra Plan. The context meter is a bare percentage with a tooltip. The "+" is still a mark with nothing behind it; attachments would make it a button.
5. **Settings** (done): the navigation moves into the sidebar's pane, grouped (Look, Models, Agent, Reach) with the sections still to come listed dimmed as "soon"; with the sidebar put away the sections are a row of pills above the page. Each page is a serif head with at most one primary action, then cards of rows (`settings_kit.rs`). Connections are rows with a switch: off (`Connection::enabled`, `Store::set_connection_enabled`) keeps the key, the models and every chat's choice, and leaves the connection out of the model menu and `Store::resolve`, so its chats fall back until it is on again. The new-connection form folds away behind "New connection" once there is one. Blur and Dim are sliders that apply as they are dragged. Search, Data folder and About from the canvas wait until they have something to do, as do its "Model for new chats" and "Effort for new chats" rows (the composer on a new chat sets both today).
6. **Themes** (done): WackCode 2's ten ported from the legacy source (never its data files). The engine keeps a theme by name (`wack_core::ThemeName`; an unknown name reads as WackCode) in place of theme colour and accent; `theme::palette` holds each one's background, accent and sweep. Dark is WackCode 2's: the window is the theme's background and the accent its accent, exactly. Light carries the background's hue, as strongly, onto the light ramp; a grey accent (Mono) mirrors to near-black. The four sweeps paint the accent's fills through `Theme::accent_fill` (send, primary pills and buttons, New chat's circle, switches that are on, slider fills); lines, text, the selection and the terminal cursor stay solid. GPUI draws two-stop gradients, so for now a sweep runs first stop to last in OKLab, missing each middle stop by a visible 0.03 to 0.05 (OKLab). Settings › Appearance shows the ten as small windows drawn in their own tokens. The gallery takes `--look <theme>|all`.
7. **Picture library** (done): several pictures, one shown or none, each remembering the theme last used with it: showing a picture brings its theme back, and choosing a theme while it shows is remembered on it (`Appearance::show`, `choose_theme`, `add`, `remove` in `wack-core/appearance.rs`). A settings file with the single `backdrop` of before opens as a library of one. Settings › Appearance shows the library as a strip (No picture, each picture captioned with its theme, Add a picture…); blur, dim, texture and focus apply to the picture showing. The store keeps every picture's copies and lets go of one whose copy has gone. No shuffle, and no link from a theme back to a picture.

## Open with the owner

- **Three-stop sweeps.** WackCode 2's sweeps have three stops; GPUI paints two, and the two-stop sweep visibly misses the middle stop. Painting all three (in masked spans, as the Ultra prism does) means reworking hover at every fill site.
- **Blur, dim and texture per picture.** They are kept on each picture, so switching pictures changes them; WackCode 2 had one setting for all pictures. Whether they should be shared.
